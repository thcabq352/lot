INT. TENT - NIGHT

A bare-face clown waits by the trunk. The mask is in her hands.

ADA (quietly)
Don't put it on.

BO
You said that last year.

EXT. MIDWAY - NIGHT

Paper boats of light. A wheel turns with nobody on it.

BO
She's gone.

INT. GATE - DAWN

The mask is on the ground. Dawn finds the tent ropes first.
